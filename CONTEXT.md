# CurrentJabe

CurrentJabe collects anonymous community electricity observations and turns them into area-level status, history and cautious forecasts.

## Language

**Report**:
One contributor's submitted observation about electricity status or a completed outage time.
_Avoid_: Prediction, public report row

**Reported outage window**:
A privacy-safe time interval aggregated from one or more usable reports. A singleton window is still visible.
_Avoid_: Prediction, schedule

**Emerging pattern**:
A recurring time pattern supported by sparse evidence and explicitly not presented as a forecast.
_Avoid_: Weak forecast, probability

**Community forecast**:
A forward-looking estimate supported by repeated, recent reports from a sufficiently broad group of contributors.
_Avoid_: Official schedule, prediction lock

**Live status**:
Recent area-level agreement about whether electricity appears on or out. Historical outage windows do not determine it.
_Avoid_: Forecast, silence means on
